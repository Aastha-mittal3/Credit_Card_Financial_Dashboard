# Credit_Card_Financial_Dashboard
An interactive Power BI project featuring two dynamic dashboards: Credit Card Transaction Analysis and Credit Card Customer Analysis. This project provides actionable insights into revenue trends, customer demographics, spending behaviour, and card performance to support data-driven decision-making in the financial sector.
#Project Objective:
To enable strategic decision-making by providing clear, actionable insights into customer spending behavior, product usage, and revenue contribution patterns for credit card services.
#Dashboards:
1. **Credit Card Transaction Analysis**
  Provides a detailed breakdown of:
- Revenue, interest, and transaction volume by card type
- Quarterly trends in revenue and transaction frequency
- Spending categories such as bills, travel, fuel, and entertainment
- Revenue patterns across customer segments like age, education, income group, and gender
2. **Credit Card Customer Analysis**
  Focuses on:
- Revenue and interest generated by gender, job type, and income
- Time-series analysis of gender-wise revenue
- Demographic segmentation: Age, Marital Status, Education, State, and Dependency
- Revenue contribution by income group across quarters
#Tools & Technologies Used::
- **Power BI** – Data visualization, DAX modeling, dashboard design
- **MySQL** – Data storage, transformation, and querying
